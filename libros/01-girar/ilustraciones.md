# Libro 1 · Tomi aprende a girar · Ilustraciones

Un prompt por página (las dobles llevan una sola imagen panorámica). Siguen el texto de `texto.md` con los cambios aprobados el 25 de octubre de 2026. Estilo elegido: **A, papel recortado**.

## Cómo usar estos prompts

Cada prompt se arma así: **[ESTILO] + [TOMI] + [RULA] + la escena + [REGLAS]**. Los bloques fijos están abajo, en inglés, porque los generadores de imágenes responden mejor así.

- **[ESTILO]:** el bloque fijo de abajo (dirección A, papel recortado).
- **Formato:** dobles en proporción 2:1 (`--ar 2:1` o "wide 2:1 panoramic"); páginas solas en 1:1.
- Generá primero una **hoja de personaje** de Tomi y otra de Rula, y usalas como imagen de referencia en todas las páginas para que no cambien.

### Bloques fijos

**[ESTILO]**
```
cut-paper collage illustration, hand-painted textured paper, visible brush strokes and paper grain, warm saturated colors on cream background, soft irregular edges, no outlines, motion arrows and dashed lines made of thick dark navy cut-paper strips, children's picture book
```

**[TOMI]**
```
Tomi, a 5-month-old baby boy, very small with a big round head, chubby cheeks with pink blush, large round dark eyes, light olive skin, almost bald except for one tuft of dark brown hair standing up above his forehead, toothless smile, wearing a plain leaf-green short-sleeved bodysuit, barefoot
```

**[RULA]**
```
Rula, a large soft rubber play ball about 35 cm in diameter (almost as big as the baby's torso), six vertical color segments in fixed order red, orange, yellow, leaf green, sky blue, violet, slightly worn with a tiny scuff on the yellow segment, no face
```

**[REGLAS]**
```
baby is always on the floor on a soft knitted blanket, no bed, no sofa, no changing table, no small objects near the baby, an adult partially visible nearby whenever the baby is on his tummy, nothing important in the center fold of the spread, leave a calm empty area for text, no text, no letters, no numbers in the image
```

## Página 1 · Portada interior

**Qué se ve:** Tomi a medio giro, de costado, con Rula rodando adelante.

```
[ESTILO]. [TOMI] lying on his side mid-roll on a soft knitted blanket on a living room floor, one arm reaching forward, determined face with the tip of his tongue out. In front of him, [RULA] rolling away, with two short curved motion lines behind it. Low camera at floor level. Simple cream background with a hint of wooden floor. Large empty area in the top third for the book title. [REGLAS]. Square 1:1.
```

## Páginas 2–3 · Doble 1. Presentación

**Qué se ve:** Tomi boca arriba sobre una manta en el piso del living. Luz de mañana. Rula al costado.

```
[ESTILO]. Wide living room scene at floor level. [TOMI] lying on his back on a soft knitted blanket on the floor, looking up curiously, arms and legs in the air. On the right side of the blanket, [RULA] resting still. Warm morning light coming through a window, a plant, the legs of a low sofa in the background, an adult's slippers and legs partially visible at the edge of the page. Tomi on the left page, Rula on the right page, center fold clear. Calm area at the top of each page for text. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 4–5 · Doble 2. Arriba

**Qué se ve:** lo que ve Tomi boca arriba: el techo, la lámpara y la cara de mamá. Lugar para la palabra ARRIBA y una flecha (se agregan en la maqueta).

```
[ESTILO]. Point of view of a baby lying on his back on the floor, looking straight up. We see the ceiling, a round hanging lamp, and the smiling face of mom leaning in from the edge of the frame (curly hair tied up, round glasses). The tips of Tomi's own tiny hands visible at the bottom edge. Soft morning light. Large empty space on the upper right page for a big word and an upward arrow to be added later. Lamp on the left page, mom's face on the right page, center fold clear. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 6–7 · Doble 3. Rula

**Qué se ve:** primer plano de Rula. Tomi la mira de reojo, con la mano estirada.

```
[ESTILO]. Close-up at floor level. On the right page, [RULA] large and bright, filling much of the page, resting on the blanket. On the left page, [TOMI] lying on his back, head turned sideways, eyes looking at the ball, one small hand stretched toward it but not reaching. Soft knitted blanket texture. Center fold clear, the gap between his hand and the ball falls just right of the fold. Calm area at the top for text. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 8–9 · Doble 4. El esfuerzo

**Qué se ve:** tres viñetas chicas: brazo estirado, pierna levantada, cabeza girada. Cara de concentración.

> Los números 1, 2 y 3 de cada viñeta se agregan en la maqueta, arriba de cada una.

```
[ESTILO]. Three small rounded panels in a row across the spread, same framing in each, top-down view of [TOMI] on his back on the blanket. Panel 1: one arm stretched out to the side as far as it goes. Panel 2: one leg lifted high and crossing over. Panel 3: head turned to the side, tip of the tongue out, concentrated face. [RULA] partially visible at the right edge of each panel, just out of reach. Panel 1 on the left page, panel 3 on the right page, panel 2 split so that no face sits on the center fold. Small empty space above each panel. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 10–11 · Doble 5. El giro

**Qué se ve:** Tomi de costado. Una línea punteada suave de la cabeza a los pies (el eje) y una flecha curva alrededor, como un palo de amasar que rueda.

```
[ESTILO]. [TOMI] lying on his side on the blanket, mid-roll from back to tummy, head and shoulders leading, hips following, eyes wide with surprise. A soft dashed dark navy line runs straight through his body from the top of his head to his feet, showing the axis of rotation. A big curved arrow wraps around the dashed line showing the direction of the roll. In a small corner inset, a wooden rolling pin rolling on a table with the same dashed axis and curved arrow. Tomi spans both pages lengthwise with his torso on the right page, so no face on the center fold. Calm area at the top for text. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 12–13 · Doble 6. Abajo

**Qué se ve:** Tomi boca abajo, sonriente, apoyado en los antebrazos. Lugar para la palabra ABAJO y una flecha (se agregan en la maqueta).

> **Nota:** boca abajo siempre con un adulto cerca: se ven las rodillas de papá al costado.

```
[ESTILO]. [TOMI] lying on his tummy on the knitted blanket, propped up on his forearms, head lifted, big proud open-mouthed smile. Low camera close to the floor. The blanket pattern and wooden floor clearly visible under him. Dad's knees and hands (mustard sweater sleeve) visible at the edge of the right page, sitting on the floor nearby. A few tiny comic "bounce" lines near Tomi to suggest he just landed softly. Large empty space at the bottom of the left page for a big word and a downward arrow to be added later. Tomi on the right page, center fold clear. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 14–15 · Doble 7. Lo redondo rueda

**Qué se ve:** Rula rodando con líneas de movimiento. Su punto de apoyo en el piso marcado con una estrellita.

```
[ESTILO]. On the left page, [TOMI] on his tummy on the blanket, one hand just touching the ball. On the right page, [RULA] rolling away across the wooden floor, its color segments shown turning, short motion lines behind it. Where the ball touches the floor, one single small bright star marks the tiny contact point. A faded ghost of the ball where it started, to show the path. Center fold clear between hand and ball. Calm area at the top for text. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 16–17 · Doble 8. Lo plano no rueda

**Qué se ve:** el cubo apoyado sobre una cara entera. Tomi lo empuja con cara de sorpresa.

```
[ESTILO]. [TOMI] on his tummy on the blanket, hand pushing a large soft fabric toy block (bigger than his head, rounded edges, one bold color per side). The block sits flat and still on one whole face, its full bottom face highlighted with a soft colored band where it touches the floor, contrasting with the single star of the previous page. Tomi's face shows surprise, eyebrows up, mouth in an "o". [RULA] far in the background, out of focus. Tomi on the left page, block on the right page, center fold clear. Calm area at the top for text. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 18–19 · Doble 9. ¿Qué rueda y qué no?

**Qué se ve:** vista desde arriba con una naranja, un rollo de cartón, una rueda de juguete de tela, un libro, un almohadón y el cubo, con espacio para que el chico señale.

```
[ESTILO]. Top-down view of a wooden floor with the knitted blanket on one side. Objects spread out with plenty of space between them so a child can point at each one: an orange, a cardboard tube lying on its side, a soft fabric toy wheel lying flat (round on its edge, flat on its faces, bigger than the baby's head), a closed picture book, a square cushion, and the soft fabric toy block from before. Six objects, three on each page, none on the center fold. [TOMI] on his tummy at the bottom edge of the left page, looking at them with curiosity. All objects are far from his reach. Even, bright lighting, clean background. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 20–21 · Doble 10. Uno, dos, tres y cierre

**Página izquierda:** tres Tomis en fila, cada uno con un número grande (1, 2, 3, se agregan en la maqueta). Al final, Rula.
**Página derecha:** Tomi boca abajo, levantando la cabeza y el pecho, mirando hacia arriba, a la ventana y a los estantes. Es el adelanto del libro 2 ("quiero ver todo desde más arriba").

```
[ESTILO]. Left page: a sequence of three Tomis in a row across the blanket, showing successive rolls (first on his tummy, then on his back, then on his tummy again), each a little further to the right, with a small curved arrow between each one and an empty space above each Tomi for a large number. At the end of the row, [RULA], and the last Tomi's hand touching it with joy. Right page: [TOMI] alone on his tummy on the blanket, pushing up on his arms, head and chest lifted high, looking up toward a bright window and high shelves with toys he cannot see from the floor, with a hopeful, curious face. On the floor, his soft shadow in the morning light faintly looks like a baby sitting up. Mom's feet partially visible at the edge. Calm area at the top of the right page for text. Center fold clear. [REGLAS]. Wide 2:1 spread.
```

## Páginas 22–23 · Experimento: ¿Qué rueda y qué no?

**Qué se ve:** dibujos chicos (viñetas) para acompañar materiales y pasos. La tabla se diseña en la maqueta, no en la imagen. Acá aparece un nene de 5 a 7 años (el lector), no Tomi bebé.

```
[ESTILO]. Set of small separate spot illustrations on a cream background, each isolated with empty space around it: (1) a homemade low, gentle ramp made of a large book resting on one or two other books; (2) a group of objects: a play ball, an orange, an empty toilet paper tube, a jar lid, a small cardboard box and an empty closed plastic bottle; (3) a child about 6 years old kneeling at the top of the ramp, releasing an orange without pushing, with an adult hand nearby; (4) the cardboard tube shown twice, once lying on its side and once standing upright, with a small question mark area left empty; (5) a jar lid shown once on its edge and once lying flat. No text, no letters, no numbers in the image.
```

## Página 24 · Para los grandes

**Qué se ve:** una viñeta chica y tranquila. Es una página de texto.

```
[ESTILO]. Small calm spot illustration at the bottom corner: [TOMI] asleep on his back in a simple crib with a firm flat mattress and fitted sheet, no pillows, no stuffed toys, no loose blankets, no bumpers. [RULA] resting on the floor next to the crib, outside it. Soft night colors. Lots of empty space above for text. No text, no letters, no numbers in the image. Square 1:1.
```

> Esta viñeta muestra la regla de dormir boca arriba que el texto menciona en "Seguridad".
