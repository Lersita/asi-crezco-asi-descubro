# KDP step by step: Book 1 (paperback)

How to upload **Tommy Learns to Roll Over** (English). The Spanish edition, **Tommy aprende a girar**, follows exactly the same steps: only the language, texts, and files change (they are listed at the end).

Files you need:

- Interior: `libro1-en-interior.pdf`
- Cover: `libro1-en-tapa.pdf`

KDP saves your progress on every page, so you can stop and come back from your **Bookshelf** at any time.

---

## Step 0 · Start a new paperback

1. Go to **kdp.amazon.com** and open your **Bookshelf**.
2. Click **+ Create**.
3. Choose **Paperback** and click **Create paperback**.

You'll go through 3 pages: **Paperback Details**, **Paperback Content**, and **Paperback Rights & Pricing**.

>

## Page 2 · Paperback Content

### Print ISBN

1. Choose **Get a free KDP ISBN** and click **Assign ISBN**.
2. KDP warns you that it can't be changed later: confirm.
3. **Imprint:** it will say *Independently published*. Leave it.

**Publication Date:** leave empty.

### Print Options

| Field | What to choose |
| --- | --- |
| **Ink and Paper Type** | **Premium Color ink, White paper** |
| **Trim Size** | Click **Select a different size** → **8.5 x 8.5 in** (21.59 x 21.59 cm) |
| **Bleed Settings** | **Bleed (PDF only)** |
| **Paperback cover finish** | **Matte** |
| **Reading direction** | Left to right |

### Manuscript

Click **Upload paperback manuscript** and choose **`libro1-en-interior.pdf`**. Wait until it says it was uploaded successfully (it can take a few minutes: the file is about 32 MB).

### Book Cover

1. Choose **Upload a cover you already have (print-ready PDF only)**.
2. Click **Upload your cover file** and choose **`libro1-en-tapa.pdf`**.
3. KDP adds the barcode on the back cover by itself. The space for it, bottom right of the back cover, is already empty.

### AI-Generated Content

KDP asks: **Did you use AI tools in creating texts, images, and/or translations in your book?** Answer **Yes** and fill in:

| Type | What to choose | Tool to name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Texts** | Entire work, with extensive editing (if you rewrote the text yourself) or with minimal or no editing (if you kept it as it is) | Claude (Anthropic) |
| **Images** | Entire work, with extensive editing | Nano Banana Pro and Nano Banana 2 (Google), via Kie.ai |
| **Translations** | Entire work, with extensive editing or with minimal or no editing (same as for texts) | Claude (Anthropic) |

This information is only for Amazon: it is not shown on the book's page.

### Book Preview

1. Click **Launch Previewer** and wait until it opens.
2. Turn the pages and check:
   - nothing important is cut at the edges or in the center;
   - the texts are not too close to the edges.
3. If the previewer shows **issues** (for example, "text outside margins" or "low image resolution"), take a screenshot and send it to me before you approve.
4. If everything looks right, click **Approve**.

### Summary

Under **Summary** you'll see the **Printing cost** (USD 4.20 for book 1). Click **Save and Continue**.

### Before publishing: order a printed proof

1. On this page, look for **Request printed proofs** (it may also be on the Bookshelf, in the "…" menu next to the book).
2. Order **1 copy**. You only pay printing and shipping.
3. Note: if KDP doesn't ship proofs to Argentina, you'll need a US address (for example, a package-forwarding service). Tell me if that happens.
4. When the proof arrives, check colors, sharpness, and cutting. If everything is fine, come back and click **Publish Your Paperback Book**.

If you prefer not to wait for the proof, you can click **Publish Your Paperback Book** right away. Amazon reviews the book within 72 hours and emails you when it's live.
